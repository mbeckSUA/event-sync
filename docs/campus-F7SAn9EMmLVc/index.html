Campus Happenings

Everything going on around the Aliso Viejo campus.

Pulled from Coursedog production, refreshed automatically. Excludes anything genuinely marked Private (Coursedog redacts these server-side before they even reach this data), room setup/teardown blocks, and Academic Calendar entries — those aren't "happenings" in the sense this view is for.

Facility notice is a hand-tuned guess (matches names containing "set-up," "installation," or gallery prep/paint/clean) flagging things like a closed gallery rather than an attendable event — it'll misfire as more data comes in unless these get a real, consistent tag or naming convention in Coursedog itself.

Click any event to expand it — full description, image, organizing department, and contact person when Coursedog has them on file.

Multi-day or recurring items show their first date; a ×N tag means it repeats N times in this window.
