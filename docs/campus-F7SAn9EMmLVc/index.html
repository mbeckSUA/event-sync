Pulled from Coursedog production Sept 4, 2026 (124 real events, full Sept 4–Dec 1 window). Excludes anything genuinely marked Private (Coursedog redacts these server-side before they even reach this data), internal meetings, room setup/teardown blocks, external rentals, individual student space-booking requests, and one obvious test record — those aren't "happenings" in the sense this view is for. Academic-calendar milestones (holidays, deadlines) are included, tagged Milestone.

Facility notice is a hand-tuned guess (matches names containing "set-up," "installation," or gallery prep/paint/clean) flagging things like a closed gallery rather than an attendable event — it'll misfire as more data comes in unless these get a real, consistent tag or naming convention in Coursedog itself.

Multi-day or recurring items show their first date; a ×N tag means it repeats N times in this window.
